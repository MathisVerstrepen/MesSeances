## Changed

- Film page chain logos now open the chain's film page in a new tab when a verified URL exists, while cinema names keep their MesSeances cinema links ([#255](https://github.com/MathisVerstrepen/MesSeances/pull/255)).

## Added

- Provider trailer fallback for films without TMDB trailers uses CGR and Cineville metadata and proxy-based UGC lookup, renders click-to-load MP4 or YouTube players, and existing TMDB and admin trailers still take precedence ([#256](https://github.com/MathisVerstrepen/MesSeances/pull/256)).
- Verified provider film page URLs are captured during sync for CGR, Grand Ecran, Noé Cinémas, Ciné-Movida, Véo, Kinepolis, Cineville and MK2, with validated direct routes for UGC, Pathé, Cinéode, Ociné, Megarama, Majestic and Cinewest/Cine Office; sitemap failures preserve the previous snapshot ([#255](https://github.com/MathisVerstrepen/MesSeances/pull/255)).

## Improved

- TMDB enrichment uses a separate 10-minute `SYNC_ENRICHMENT_TIMEOUT` and retries transient I/O and 500/502/503/504 failures with up to three attempts ([#256](https://github.com/MathisVerstrepen/MesSeances/pull/256)).
- Sync recovery scopes proxy failures per request and retries Pathé acquisition once when a future programme references a show missing from the earlier catalog ([#256](https://github.com/MathisVerstrepen/MesSeances/pull/256)).
- Frontend browser CI timeout increased from 15 to 25 minutes.

## Fixed

- Cinéode sync accepts published poster URLs with uppercase `.JPG` extensions and `%20` spaces while preserving original URLs ([#252](https://github.com/MathisVerstrepen/MesSeances/pull/252)).
- Brotli and compression dependencies updated to address vulnerabilities ([#252](https://github.com/MathisVerstrepen/MesSeances/pull/252)).
- Recognized cinema event labels and TMDB title aliases no longer block film matches; ligatures and `&`/`and`/`et` compare as equivalent without changing stored titles ([#254](https://github.com/MathisVerstrepen/MesSeances/pull/254)).
- Ociné keeps validated programme rows and booking links through transient checkout failures, MK2 keeps the first slug for duplicate film IDs, and Véo keeps a booking with an unknown room when only room metadata conflicts ([#256](https://github.com/MathisVerstrepen/MesSeances/pull/256)).
